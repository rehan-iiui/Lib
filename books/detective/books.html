The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Arthur Conan Doyle

Sherlock Holmes is a brilliant detective who solves difficult mysteries using careful observation, logic, and deduction. His friend Dr. John Watson records many of their exciting cases.

The book contains several mysteries involving strange clues, missing objects, secret plans, and unusual crimes. Holmes carefully studies every detail to discover the truth.

The Hound of the Baskervilles

Arthur Conan Doyle

Sherlock Holmes investigates the mysterious death of Sir Charles Baskerville, which appears to be connected to an old family legend about a terrifying supernatural hound.

Holmes and Dr. Watson travel to the gloomy Baskerville estate and investigate strange events, hidden secrets, and suspicious people while trying to discover whether the hound is real.

The Sign of the Four

Arthur Conan Doyle

Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson become involved in a mystery surrounding a missing treasure, a secret message, and the disappearance of a woman's father.

The investigation takes Holmes through London as he follows clues, studies evidence, and uncovers a complicated story involving friendship, betrayal, and hidden treasure.

The Murder of Roger Ackroyd

Agatha Christie

Roger Ackroyd is found dead in his home shortly after receiving information about a mysterious secret. The quiet village suddenly becomes the center of a complicated murder investigation.

Detective Hercule Poirot examines the evidence and questions the people connected to Ackroyd. As the investigation continues, hidden secrets begin to appear and the mystery becomes increasingly difficult to solve.

Murder on the Orient Express

Agatha Christie

Hercule Poirot is traveling aboard the famous Orient Express when a passenger is found murdered during the journey. A heavy snowfall prevents the train from continuing.

Poirot interviews the passengers and carefully compares their stories. Almost everyone appears to have a possible connection to the crime, creating one of his most famous mysteries.

The Mystery of the Blue Train

Agatha Christie

A wealthy woman is found dead while traveling on the luxurious Blue Train, and a valuable collection of jewels has disappeared.

Hercule Poirot investigates the passengers and follows a series of clues to discover who was responsible. The case involves secrets, disguises, and valuable jewels.

The Moonstone

Wilkie Collins

The Moonstone is a valuable diamond that disappears from an English country house after being given to Rachel Verinder as a birthday present.

Several people become suspects, and different investigators attempt to discover what happened. The mystery unfolds through clues, testimonies, and surprising discoveries.

The Maltese Falcon

Dashiell Hammett

Private detective Sam Spade becomes involved in a dangerous search for a valuable statue called the Maltese Falcon.

Several people are searching for the same object, and Spade must determine who is telling the truth. The investigation becomes filled with deception, secrets, and unexpected turns.

The Westing Game

Ellen Raskin

Sixteen people are brought together after the mysterious death of wealthy businessman Samuel Westing. His unusual will creates a contest in which the participants must solve a mystery to inherit his fortune.

The players form teams and search for clues hidden throughout the story. As they investigate, they discover that appearances can be misleading and that everyone has secrets.

The Mysterious Affair at Styles

Agatha Christie

Emily Inglethorp is found dead at Styles Court, and suspicion falls on several people living in the house. Hercule Poirot is asked to investigate the mysterious circumstances.

Poirot carefully examines the evidence, studies everyone's statements, and uses logic to uncover the truth behind the murder. This book introduces Hercule Poirot as one of fiction's most famous detectives.

The Big Sleep

Raymond Chandler

Philip Marlowe is a private detective working in Los Angeles.

He is hired by the wealthy General Sternwood.

The general wants Marlowe to investigate a blackmail problem.

The case involves the general's younger daughter, Carmen.

Marlowe soon discovers that the situation is more complicated than expected.

He also learns about the disappearance of a man named Sean Regan.

While investigating, Marlowe encounters several suspicious people.

Each person seems to have something they want to hide.

The detective follows clues through different parts of Los Angeles.

He investigates photographs, mysterious messages, and strange connections.

Some of the evidence leads him toward a group of dangerous criminals.

Marlowe must carefully decide which witnesses are telling the truth.

Several events that appear unrelated gradually become connected.

The investigation becomes increasingly dangerous for Marlowe.

He continues searching for answers despite threats and deception.

His observations help him uncover important details.

The case reveals secrets involving money, relationships, and crime.

Marlowe eventually brings together the clues he has discovered.

The story is known for its complex mystery and hard-boiled detective style.

The Murder at the Vicarage

Agatha Christie

The story takes place in the peaceful English village of St. Mary Mead.

Colonel Lucius Protheroe is a wealthy and influential member of the village.

He is not especially popular with many of the local residents.

One day, Colonel Protheroe is found murdered inside the vicar's study.

The shocking discovery quickly attracts the attention of the police.

Several people in the village have possible reasons to dislike the colonel.

The investigation begins by examining the people who knew him.

The local vicar becomes closely involved in the mystery.

Different suspects provide different versions of what happened.

Their stories contain details that do not always seem to agree.

Miss Marple carefully watches the behavior of the villagers.

She understands the personalities and relationships within the community.

Her observations allow her to notice things that others overlook.

As the investigation continues, more secrets begin to come to light.

The mystery becomes more complicated as new clues are discovered.

Miss Marple compares the evidence with what she knows about the villagers.

She uses careful reasoning rather than simply trusting appearances.

Eventually, the important clues are connected together.

The mystery reaches a surprising conclusion and reveals the identity of the murderer.
